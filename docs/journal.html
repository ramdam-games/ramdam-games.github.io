Améliorations

  • A body down is not measured while none of it is seen, and asleep and still only one picture in four

  • The limbs of an animal no camera sees, or sees from too far to tell its legs apart, stay at rest
  • A plot out of every camera's view is not handed to the engine, and each plot has its own box
  • An animal with its bones at rest is drawn by a plain copy of its mesh
  • The picture is worked on in 32 bits a pixel rather than 64
  • The looks ahead of every animal reuse one list of hits instead of a new one at each step
  • The size fitted to each line of text is looked up without writing a new string every picture
  • Light smooths the edges in one pass over the picture, the temporal smoothing stays at Normal
  • The plain copies of the skins at rest are taken out again, they brought 1 % on average and nothing at Light

  • Shorter wordings where a sign or a plate was too small for them
  • The words of the hoardings keep to their white
  • Every text keeps a margin of 6 % on each side of what carries it, and the check measures against that room
  • A name or a price in Latin letters keeps the face of the game in every language, so the plates, the permit and the sums are drawn alike
  • The ground under a lying body is felt from the feet outwards, so a table top, a bench or the floor of the truck over the head is not taken for ground
  • A colleague's body that comes into view while he sleeps lies there at once, instead of standing up and going down again
  • The stream is laid after the truck has carried its passengers and the bodies have been placed, so it leaves the hips on a moving truck
  • A word of katakana is not broken in two when the line can break elsewhere
  • The flamingo keeps its legs still, the leg it holds up crossed the other at every stride
  • The elephant and the hippopotamus step twice a stride of the toy
  • An animal is in sight when it stands in a camera's view, its shadow counted, not when the engine says it drew it
  • Only a wall takes the speed of the legs away, a player standing still got going again at a high frame rate
  • The plots are chosen after the cameras are placed, with the view of the picture about to be drawn
  • What is carried, stuck in front of the player, takes the speed of the legs into it away, as a wall does
